# Notes on the Dashboard

Standalone Notes have no panel of their own: they live on the Dashboard, and the filter's `Notes` link (after the Areas, set apart, before `Edit areas`) shows them alone, with the open Note count. The filter is `?show=notes`, never `?area=`, so an Area named "Notes" cannot collide with it; choosing either clears the other, and both survive the Thread pane and the Note view. `/notes`, `/inbox`, and `?inbox=true` land on the Dashboard filtered to Notes. Notes are archived rather than marked done: `Archive` and `Unarchive` everywhere, `Archived <date>` in the Note view. The palette's History chip lists `Resolved threads` and `Archived notes`; a search reaches every Archived Note by body on the service, not just the first page. Choosing one opens the Note view over the page, without Add to thread actions (ADR 0031).

## Sub-features

Pagination follow-up: proven with the PR 394 fix at 1440×900 and 390×844. Created and archived 21 Notes with the same body through the UI, searched for their shared word, loaded the final match by mouse on desktop and by Tab → Tab → Enter on phone, and opened the oldest Note with End → Enter. Unarchiving the oldest Note on desktop restored it to the board (`state = 'open'`, `completed_at = null`). Evidence: `.verify/evidence/pr394-fix/2026-10-03T03-03-34-835Z/`, captures `desktop-search-before-more`, `desktop-search-after-more`, `desktop-oldest-note`, and their `phone-` equivalents.

- `notes-no-panel`: the chrome has no Notes button or badge at any width; the sky header holds the date, search, New note, New thread and the account menu (ADR 0037).
- `notes-filter`: `navigation "Filter the board"` lists `All <n>`, each Area `<name> <n>`, `No area <n>` (any that do not fit fold into `button "<n> more"`), then `link "Notes <open Note count>"` and `button "Edit areas"`. Choosing it sets `?show=notes` and shows only open Standalone Notes: dated ones under the list's day, week and month headings, undated ones in `No date` → `Notes`. Muted at zero. Empty: `No Note is asking for you.`
- `notes-filter-collision`: an Area named `Notes` is its own link (`Notes 0`, slug `notes-<hex>`) among the Areas; it sets `?area=notes-<hex>` and says `Nothing open in Notes.`
- `notes-filter-survives`: opening a Note keeps `?show=notes`; opening a Thread from the palette gives `?show=notes&thread=<slug>`, and closing it returns to `?show=notes`.
- `notes-redirects`: `/notes` and `/inbox` land on `/?show=notes`; `/threads/<slug>?inbox=true` lands on `/?thread=<slug>&show=notes`.
- `notes-archive`: a card's `Archive note` and the Note view's `Archive` both answer `Note archived` and take the Note off the board; the view stays open with `Archived <Mon d>` and `Unarchive`. D1 stores `state = 'done'` and `completed_at`.
- `notes-archive-repeating`: a repeating Note's card offers `Complete · back <Mmm d>` instead of `Archive note` (see [Follow-up dates](./follow-up-dates.md)); Archive is in its Note view, where it ends the rhythm. An archived repeating Note keeps its date and Repeat (`notes.repeat`), is found in History by its body, and `Unarchive` returns it at that date with its glyph and Complete.
- `history-archived`: the palette's `History` chip shows `group "Resolved threads"` then `group "Archived notes"`, the latter 20 most recently archived first, each `option "<one-line preview> <Mon d>"`. A search narrows both groups; Archived notes come from the service, so an older Note past the first page is found by a word of its body.
- `history-more-matches`: a nonempty search with more matches shows `button "Load more archived notes"`. Activating it appends the next page and returns focus to the search, preserving the query. Every matching Note is reachable even when more than 20 share the same body. An empty search stays bounded to one page.
- `history-open-note`: choosing an Archived note opens `dialog "Note"` over the page with `Archived <Mon d>`, `Unarchive`, and a ⋯ menu of only Copy Markdown and Delete note. `Unarchive` answers `Note unarchived` and returns the Note to the board.
- `notes-thread-capture`: `New thread` under `?show=notes` opens with `Add area` (no Area).
- `notes-palette-go-to`: the palette's Go to → `Notes` sets `?show=notes`.
- `notes-phone`: at 390×844 the same row shows what fits (often `All`), `button "<n> more"` with the rest as `menuitemradio` plus `Edit areas`, then `Notes <n>` and `Edit areas`. Notes is always in the row.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click `Notes` near the end of the Dashboard's filter, or choose Go to → Notes in the palette. It shows on a phone too.
- Hover a Note card and click its archive control, or open a Note and click `Archive`.
- Open the palette (Command K or the `Search, Command K` field in the header, or in the bottom bar on a phone), click `History`, and type a word from the Note.

## Gotchas

- With an Area named `Notes`, two links read `Notes <n>`. The Area's sits among the Areas (or in the More menu); the filter's comes after them, before `Edit areas`. Use `--exact` with the count, or the order.
- `Archive note` with `--exact` matches the first card on the board. Undated Notes read newest-first, so repeated clicks archive the newest first, which makes the first-archived Note the oldest in History.
- History's empty search reads one page of 20. A nonempty search offers `Load more archived notes` for further matches. The search is debounced by 250 ms, so wait for the result. A cached search may already contain all loaded pages and no longer show the button; reload before verifying pagination from its first page.
- `Thread actions` can be clicked before the pane renders, leaving no menu. Click it again after the pane's title shows.
- Adding to a Thread waits out a five-second Undo window before it writes; prove it after a reload. The toast says `Note added to thread`.
- On a phone the Note view's header shows `Archived` without the date to leave room for the header actions.
